FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

You Have Questions.
We’re Here to Help.

What types of therapy and consulting do you offer?

Home Field Consulting lists individual, couples, and family therapy, teletherapy, consultation, and speaking services. Contact the practice to discuss current availability and which service may fit.

Do you offer virtual sessions?

Teletherapy is listed among the practice’s services. Availability and eligibility may depend on your location and the clinician’s licensing.

What can I expect in a consultation?

A consultation offers a chance to discuss your goals, ask questions, and learn about possible next steps. Contact the practice for exact scheduling and format details.

How do I know if therapy or consulting is right for me?

The team can help clarify your needs and talk through whether a clinical therapy service or consulting approach is appropriate.

Do you work with athletes and high performers?

Dr. Nicolas describes her work as sports family therapy. Reach out to discuss your specific athletic, family, or performance-related goals.
